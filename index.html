Our Story

A Small Shop With a Big Love for Coffee

Brew & Bean started with one simple idea: serve coffee the way it should taste — fresh beans, ground seconds before brewing, and milk steamed to silk. No shortcuts, no stale pots sitting on warmers.

Our beans come from small farms and are roasted in small batches each week, so every cup tastes alive. Pair it with a croissant baked in-house this morning and you have the best part of your day.

  • Beans roasted in-house every week
  • Bakery items baked fresh each morning
  • Oat, almond and soy milk at no extra charge
  • Free Wi-Fi and plenty of power sockets
